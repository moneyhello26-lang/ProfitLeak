# Taraz cafe demo data

The small CSV files in this directory illustrate the import templates. The full
dataset is generated by `backend/scripts/generate_demo.py` into `taraz_90d/` and
is also available through `POST /api/v1/demo/load`.

Version `taraz-cafe-v1` uses the fixed period 2026-07-04 through 2026-10-01. Dates
and values never depend on the current date or a random seed. The demo loader
uses a stable business UUID and the importer to preserve provenance and prevent
duplicates. All records are synthetic.

## Independent expected calculations

Every day has 25 coffees at 1,200 KZT, 18 teas at 700 KZT and 10 sandwiches at
1,800 KZT. Daily revenue is 30,000 + 12,600 + 18,000 = 60,600 KZT. Over 90 days,
revenue is **5,454,000 KZT**.

Coffee cost is 450 KZT per unit for the first 60 days and 900 for the final 30.
Tea cost is 250 and sandwich cost is 800 throughout. First period daily COGS is
11,250 + 4,500 + 8,000 = 23,750 KZT; final period daily COGS is 22,500 + 4,500 +
8,000 = 35,000 KZT. Total COGS is 60 × 23,750 + 30 × 35,000 = **2,475,000 KZT**.

Gross profit is **2,979,000 KZT**, with gross margin **54.62%**. Packaging totals
106,500 KZT, wages 135,000, utilities 45,000 and rent 450,000. Total operating
expenses are **736,500 KZT**; operating profit is **2,242,500 KZT**.

The fixture `taraz_expected.json` stores these expected values; tests compare the
database/API results against it.

## Detector scenarios

- Compare the final 30 days (2026-09-02 through 2026-10-01) with the preceding
  30 days (2026-08-03 through 2026-09-01). Packaging grows from 30,000 to 46,500
  KZT, a 55% increase. The current total includes one possible duplicate.
- Coffee gross margin falls from 62.50% to 25.00% as unit cost doubles, while
  price and sales quantity stay constant.
- Two packaging expense rows on 2026-10-01 have matching category, description
  and amount of 1,500 KZT. They remain distinct records with source rows so they
  can be reviewed by a person.

Run `POST /api/v1/analysis/run` for the demo business to persist these three
signals. Packaging scenario savings are 46,500 − 30,000 = **16,500 KZT**.
Coffee scenario savings are 900,000 × (62.50 − 25.00) / 100 = **337,500 KZT**,
assuming restored baseline margin at current revenue. Duplicate savings are null
until a person checks the source documents. Scenarios can overlap and are not
summed. See `REQUIREMENTS.md` for thresholds and coverage rules.
